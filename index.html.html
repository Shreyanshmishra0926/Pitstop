About PitStop

We Got Tired of
Dirty Highway Toilets.

In December 2023, four college friends drove Delhi to Goa. 1,900 kilometers, two breakdowns, and not a single restroom we'd let our sisters use. We came back angry. PitStop is what we built with that anger.

"Every traveler deserves a clean stop. Every dhaba owner deserves more customers. We built PitStop to fix both."
